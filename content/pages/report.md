issueGuides:
  - caseSlug: nozzlegate
    title: Bondtech sold the nozzle as hardened
    summary: Choose this if your INDX nozzle, product page or packaging said “hardened”.
    fields:
      - label: Vilken vara, tjänst eller aktivitet?
        value: Bondtech INDX-munstycken marknadsförda som hardened
      - label: Vilket företag vill du anmäla?
        value: Bondtech AB
      - label: Gäller anmälan en fysisk butik eller plats?
        value: Kryssa i “Inte relevant”.
      - label: Gäller anmälan ett problem på nätet?
        value: Paste the exact Bondtech product-page URL you saw. If it is no longer available, use https://www.bondtech.se/ and name the page in your description.
    encounter: Choose “Reklam på en webbplats” when the hardened claim appeared online.
    encounterNote: If the wording you relied on was printed on the box, choose “Påstående på en förpackning” instead. If your report is specifically about the completed online purchase, choose “Köp via en webbplats”.
    date: Enter the date you saw the claim or placed the order in ÅÅÅÅ-MM-DD format. Do not use Bondtech’s later statement date unless that is the event you are describing.
    attachments:
      - Order confirmation or invoice showing the product and date.
      - Screenshot or archived copy of the page that used “hardened”.
      - Photograph of the box or product label, if it used the claim.
      - Bondtech’s official update or a relevant reply from the company.
  - caseSlug: payment-surcharges
    title: Bondtech added a card or PayPal fee
    summary: Choose this if the checkout or completed order included a payment-method fee.
    fields:
      - label: Vilken vara, tjänst eller aktivitet?
        value: Kort- eller PayPal-avgift i Bondtechs webbshop
      - label: Vilket företag vill du anmäla?
        value: Bondtech AB
      - label: Gäller anmälan en fysisk butik eller plats?
        value: Kryssa i “Inte relevant”.
      - label: Gäller anmälan ett problem på nätet?
        value: Paste the checkout URL shown in your browser. If it was temporary, use https://www.bondtech.se/ and state that the fee appeared at checkout.
    encounter: Choose “Köp via en webbplats”.
    encounterNote: State the country, currency, cart subtotal, payment method, exact fee label and amount. Say whether the fee changed when you switched payment method.
    date: Enter the date the checkout displayed the fee or the order date in ÅÅÅÅ-MM-DD format.
    attachments:
      - Screenshots of the same cart with each payment method selected.
      - Order confirmation or invoice showing the fee line, if the order was completed.
      - Payment receipt or statement with card and account numbers redacted.
      - Any response from Bondtech about the fee.
  - caseSlug: warranty-terms
    title: Bondtech’s terms say the warranty lasts 90 days
    summary: Choose this if that wording affected how you understood or made a complaint.
    fields:
      - label: Vilken vara, tjänst eller aktivitet?
        value: Bondtechs garanti- och reklamationsvillkor
      - label: Vilket företag vill du anmäla?
        value: Bondtech AB
      - label: Gäller anmälan en fysisk butik eller plats?
        value: Kryssa i “Inte relevant”.
      - label: Gäller anmälan ett problem på nätet?
        value: https://www.bondtech.se/shop/terms-conditions/
    encounter: Choose “Köp via en webbplats” when the terms formed part of an online purchase.
    encounterNote: If Bondtech relied on the term later in support rather than during checkout, choose “Annat” and explain where the company invoked it.
    date: Enter the purchase date, the date you read the term or the date a complaint was rejected—whichever best matches the event. Mention the other dates in your description.
    attachments:
      - Dated screenshot or PDF of the “Warranty / Complaints” wording.
      - Order confirmation or receipt.
      - Your written complaint and Bondtech’s response, if the term was applied to you.
      - Photographs or records showing the product fault, when relevant.
finish:
  files: The form accepts up to 10 files, with a limit of 10 MB per file. It accepts PDF, common document and image formats, plus MP4 and MOV. Each uploaded file needs a short description.
  affected: In “Vem är drabbad?”, choose the affected person’s age and legal sex, or use “Vill ej uppge” and “Vill inte uppge”. Select the municipality, or tick “Jag kan inte välja kommun”.
  contact: You can provide contact details or tick “Jag vill anmäla anonymt”. Always use the anonymous option if you have protected personal data, and do not upload files containing those details.
  summary: Check every field in “Sammanfattning”. When it is correct, click “Skicka min anmälan” and save the confirmation. Konsumentverket does not send personal feedback about whether a supervision case is opened.
