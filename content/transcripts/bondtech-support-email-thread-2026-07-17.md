# Bondtech support email thread, 17 July 2026

> The sender supplied the original mail files and asked for their name and
> email address to be hidden. This public copy is a transcription: the sender’s
> identity, the helpdesk ticket number, and every mail routing header and
> message identifier are removed. The Bondtech respondent is retained because
> those statements are the subject of the record.

A screenshot of the 16:41 reply in this thread circulated widely and was shown
in later video coverage. This page publishes the surrounding thread from the
sender’s own copy, including the parts the screenshot did not show: the
question that opened the ticket, the two earlier answers, and the closing
message the sender says never received a reply.

independently confirms the central product mismatch described here.

**Martin Bondéus — 17 July 2026, 16:41**

> Hi [redacted],
>
> I completely understand your disappointment, especially if you primarily
> print abrasive materials.
>
> During the development of the INDX nozzles, we evaluated a fully hardened
> (~60 HRC) steel version. However, during production we encountered
> manufacturing challenges that prevented us from machining that material
> reliably—the cutting tools simply did not withstand the process. Rather than
> releasing a product with inconsistent quality, we decided to supply the
> nitrocarburised version for the time being.
>
> The current nozzles are still surface hardened through nitrocarburising and
> offer better wear resistance than untreated steel, but they are not intended
> for prolonged use with highly abrasive filaments. This is also why we removed
> the "hardened" wording from the product page to avoid creating incorrect
> expectations.
>
> At this point, I unfortunately cannot give you a reliable timeframe for when
> a fully hardened nozzle will be available. We are actively working on a
> solution, but we don't want to promise a date until we know we can
> manufacture it consistently and to the quality we expect.
>
> I understand this is not the answer you were hoping for, and I'm sorry we
> cannot provide a more definite timeline today.
>
> Best regards,
>
> Martin Bondéus
>
> Founder
>
> Bondtech AB
