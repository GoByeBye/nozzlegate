An unassembled product needs a documented return route and confirmation of who pays. An assembled or installed product also needs a written plan for safe dismantling, packing and any related costs.   note: This is general consumer information, not legal advice. It assumes a private consumer purchase. Directives are implemented through national law, the claim normally goes to the seller on your invoice, and the exact remedy depends on the contract and local law. Verify the rules where you live before sending. A buyer outside the EU may still use Swedish remedies if Swedish law governs the purchase; citizenship alone is not the test. U.S. buyers can keep the factual refund-offer and return-plan wording, but should delete the EU and Swedish legal paragraphs unless they have confirmed that those rules apply and should check their state and federal law.
