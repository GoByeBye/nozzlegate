## Payment-method comparison

With PayPal selected, the order table shows `Fee for PayPal: 28.01 kr`.

With Credit / Debit Card selected, the same order table shows
`Fee for Credit Card (Stripe): 28.01 kr`.

The fee is approximately 2.56% of the displayed subtotal. The captures are
primary evidence of the checkout interface and the prices it presented with
Sweden selected.

## Not established by the images alone

The captures do not show a transaction date, completed order, invoice, charged
payment, or the card scheme and whether it was a consumer or corporate card.
They also do not show what happens when Prepaid (SEK) or Klarna is selected.

Because the same 28.01 kr amount appears under both the PayPal and card labels,
the images do not establish that the amount is unique to card payments. They do
establish that the checkout explicitly labels it as a credit-card fee when the
credit/debit-card option is selected.
