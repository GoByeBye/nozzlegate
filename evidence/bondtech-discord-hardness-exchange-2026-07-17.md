## Evidentiary value

The verified exchange records a company representative stating that:

- the supplied nozzles measured 30–32 on the Rockwell C scale;
- nozzles commonly marketed as hardened measure 55–60 HRC;
- the supplied tools should be treated as the system's standard-nozzle option;
- "proper hardened nozzles" would be available later;
- a free exchange was rejected at that point; and
- the representative nevertheless maintained that calling the shipped tools
  hardened was technically acceptable.

Those statements are relevant to what Bondtech knew, how it initially
characterized the difference, and how its public position developed before the
23 and 29 July statements.
